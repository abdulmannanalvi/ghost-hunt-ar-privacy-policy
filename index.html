Privacy Policy for Ghost Hunt AR

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ghost Hunt AR ("the App") handles information when you use the mobile application.

Ghost Hunt AR is an augmented reality game that uses your device's camera to provide the AR gameplay experience.

1. Information We Collect

Ghost Hunt AR does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

The App itself does not intentionally collect or store personal information. However, third-party services used by the App, including Google AdMob, may collect certain information automatically.

2. Camera Permission

Ghost Hunt AR requires access to your device's camera to provide its augmented reality gameplay.

The camera is used to display the real-world environment on your device screen and allow virtual game elements to appear within that environment.

The App does not intentionally upload or store your camera images or video on our servers.

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

Ghost Hunt AR uses Google AdMob to display interstitial advertisements.

AdMob may automatically collect or process certain information from your device and interactions with advertisements, such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app information, advertising-related information, and ad interaction information, depending on Google's services and your device settings.

This information may be used for purposes such as providing, measuring, and improving advertising, fraud prevention, and other advertising-related services.

4. Analytics and Crash Reporting

Ghost Hunt AR does not intentionally use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or other separate analytics/crash-reporting services.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Information processed by Google AdMob may be handled by Google and its service providers as necessary to provide advertising and related services.

6. Children's Privacy

Ghost Hunt AR does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information.

8. Third-Party Services

Ghost Hunt AR uses Google AdMob to display interstitial advertisements. Google may collect and process information through its Mobile Ads SDK according to Google's applicable policies and terms.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's functionality, advertising services, or data practices change.
